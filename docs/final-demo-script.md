# GrievX Campus — Final Demonstration Script

## Demonstration order

1. **Student sign-in** — register or sign in as a student and show the student home screen.
2. **Submit a complaint** — enter a title, description and manual campus location; attach an optional image.
3. **Automatic ML triage** — show the predicted category, priority and department. Explain that a confident prediction is routed immediately and uncertain or unavailable predictions automatically go to General rather than waiting for admin approval.
4. **Duplicate review** — submit a similar complaint and show the possible-duplicate warning. Demonstrate opening the existing issue and the explicit submit-anyway option for a genuinely different issue.
5. **Department portal** — sign in as a department head and show only the complaints routed to that department. Update a complaint status.
6. **Main admin dashboard** — show the campus-wide complaint queue and department activity overview. Verify the department status change is visible to the administrator.
7. **Staff directory** — show how the administrator assigns staff and department heads to existing departments.
8. **Notifications** — return to the student view and show the complaint status notification/timeline.
9. **Analytics** — show KPI totals and category, priority, department and lifecycle breakdowns; export the CSV report.

## Presentation reminders

- Use demo accounts and non-sensitive sample complaints only.
- Do not show secret keys, database credentials or private provider dashboards.
- Explain that duplicate detection is an application feature, not a fourth ML model.
- Do not claim performance metrics or test results unless they have been measured in the final test phase.
- Do not present a campus heatmap as a project feature; it is out of scope.
- Keep one backup demo account available in case a session expires.
